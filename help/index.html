Common questions

How do I set up my booking portal?

After installing SessionPro, go to Settings in the app. Your booking portal page is created automatically at /pages/booking. You can customise your branding, capacity limits, and session types from the app dashboard.

How does QR code check-in work?

Each customer receives a unique QR code after registering. At the venue, use the Check-in screen in SessionPro to scan the code — it confirms the customer's booking and marks their attendance instantly.

How do I set up membership plans?

Go to Memberships in the app and create a plan with a name, price, and billing interval. Customers can then purchase memberships through your booking portal and their status syncs automatically with Shopify.

Can customers book for multiple people?

Yes. Customers can register additional participants (for example, children) under their account and book sessions for the whole group in a single checkout.

How do waivers work?

You can require customers to sign a digital waiver before completing a booking. Signed waivers are stored securely and accessible from the customer's record in the app.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Your data is retained for 48 hours after uninstall in case you reinstall. After that, all your store's data is permanently deleted from our systems. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I need to cancel my subscription

You can cancel at any time by uninstalling SessionPro from your Shopify admin. Go to Settings → Apps → SessionPro → Delete. Your subscription ends immediately and you won't be charged again.

Something isn't working — what should I do?

Email us at support@convinewebsolutions.com with a description of the issue, your store URL, and any screenshots. We'll investigate and respond within 1 business day.
