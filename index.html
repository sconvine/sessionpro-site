A Shopify app for venues

Run your venue's sessions from your Shopify store

SessionPro handles bookings, check-in, memberships and waivers for skate rinks, indoor skateparks and other session-based venues, all connected to the Shopify store you already have.

Read the help docs Privacy Policy

Session bookings

Customers book sessions through a portal on your store, with capacity limits you control.

QR check-in

Every customer gets a unique QR code. Scan it at the door to confirm the booking and record attendance.

Memberships

Sell recurring membership plans that bill through Shopify and stay in sync automatically.

Digital waivers

Require a signed waiver before booking, and find it on the customer's record whenever you need it.
